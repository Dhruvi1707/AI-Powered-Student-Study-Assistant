## Feature 1: AI Study Planner

## Objective
To create a personalised study timetable using Artificial Intelligence based on a student's subjects, available study hours and examination date.

## Input
- Subjects: Python, DBMS and Core Java
- Available study time: 2 hours per day
- Examination: After 7 days

## Prompt Used
You are an AI-powered study planning assistant. Create a realistic 7-day study timetable for a college student with three subjects, two hours available every day and an examination after seven days. Include revision, practice questions, short breaks and difficult topics. Present the timetable in a clear table.

## Process
The AI analyses the student's requirements, divides the available time into study sessions, prioritises difficult topics and includes revision and practice activities.

## Output
A 7-day study timetable containing daily study blocks, difficult topics to prioritise, revision sessions, a mock test and tips for effective preparation.

## Result
The AI successfully generated a structured timetable covering all three subjects.

## Learning Outcome
I learned how to use clear prompts to generate a personalised study plan and how AI can assist students with time management and exam preparation.
