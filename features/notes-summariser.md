## Feature 4: AI Notes Summariser

## Objective
To create an AI-powered tool that converts lengthy study material into short, organised and easy-to-understand revision notes.

## Topic Selected
Database Management System (DBMS) – Normalization.

## Prompt Used
You are an AI-powered study notes summariser.
Summarise DBMS Normalization in beginner-friendly language. Cover the meaning and purpose of normalization, 1NF, 2NF, 3NF and BCNF. Include simple examples, important examination points and five revision questions.

## Process
The AI analyses the selected topic and organises the information into shorter explanations, headings, bullet points and examples.

## Output
Organised revision notes explaining normalization and its different normal forms, along with examples and five revision questions.

## Result
The AI successfully converted the selected topic into structured revision material that is easier to read and use for examination preparation.

## Learning Outcome
I learned how AI can simplify lengthy academic topics and convert them into organised notes for quicker revision.
