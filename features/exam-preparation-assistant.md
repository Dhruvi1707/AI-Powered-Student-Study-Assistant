## Feature 5: AI Exam Preparation Assistant

## Objective
To create an AI-powered assistant that helps students prepare for examinations through a personalised study plan.

## Subjects Selected
Python, DBMS and Core Java.

## Prompt Used
You are an AI-powered exam preparation assistant.
Create a 10-day examination preparation plan for a college IT student studying Python, DBMS and Core Java. Include important topics, daily revision, coding practice, SQL practice, mock tests, a completion checklist and practical time-management tips.

## Process
The AI analyses the student's subjects and available preparation period. It distributes topics across the 10 days and includes revision, practical exercises and mock tests.

## Output
A structured 10-day examination preparation timetable, topic checklist and five examination preparation tips.

## Result
The AI successfully generated a personalised examination preparation plan covering all three subjects.

## Learning Outcome
I learned how AI can help students organise examination preparation, prioritise important topics and manage their study time effectively.
